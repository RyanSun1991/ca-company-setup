## 四、KYC 标准答案
- **What does your business do?** "We develop and publish mobile apps. Our main product is a {{category}} app on the Apple App Store. Customers pay a subscription, and **Apple pays us monthly**."
- **Where does the money come from?** "Mainly from Apple — App Store subscription payouts, **in Canadian dollars**. The app is currently under my personal developer account; we are **transferring it to the company**, so revenue will start in a few months."
- **Expected monthly deposits?** "Small at first — **under five thousand a month**, growing over time. Mostly electronic. **No cash.**"
- **International wires?** "No regular international wires. Payments come from **Apple Canada**."
- **Source of the initial deposit?** "**My personal savings.** Today I'm depositing **${{100}} as share capital**. I may also lend the company some working capital later — as a shareholder loan."
- **Account for your own business only?** "Yes, business use only. **No third parties.**"

## 五、你要主动提出的 5 件事
1. "I'd like the **{{Essential Business Account — the $5 per month plan}}**."
2. "Please set up online banking and a business debit card."
3. "Please enable Interac e-Transfer."
4. ⭐ "**Can you write down the transit number, institution number, and account number for me?** I need them to set up Apple payouts."
5. "I'd like to deposit ${{100}} into the account today."

## 六、推销应对
- 可以要:无年费商业返现信用卡 → "Do you have a no-annual-fee business cashback Mastercard? I'd like to apply."
- 其余全拒(年费卡、透支、投资、保险)→ "Not today, thanks. Maybe later."

## 七、卡壳救急句
"Sorry, could you speak slowly, please?" / "Could you write that down for me?" / "Let me check my documents." / 给经理看这页:"I have all company details in this document."

## 八、会让你签的东西(正常)
账户协议、签字卡(以 **President** 身份)、FATCA/CRS 税务居民声明(加拿大税务居民,提供 SIN)、受益所有人声明(你 100%)。**不正常**:当场买投资/保险 → 拒绝。
