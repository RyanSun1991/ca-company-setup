## Resolution of the Sole Director of {{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}
### (the "Corporation") — Appointment of Officer

**Whereas** By-law No. 1 of the Corporation provides that the board of directors may appoint such officers as it determines, may specify their duties, and may delegate to them powers to manage the business and affairs of the Corporation;
**And whereas** the Corporation {{engages personnel assigned to it under an employer-of-record arrangement with {{EOR_LEGAL_NAME}} and}} requires an officer responsible for {{the day-to-day supervision of such personnel}};

**Be it resolved that:**
1. **Appointment.** {{OFFICER_NAME}} is appointed an officer of the Corporation with the title **"{{OFFICER_TITLE}}"**, effective **{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}**, to hold office at the pleasure of the board of directors.
2. **Duties.** The {{OFFICER_TITLE}} shall: (a) supervise and direct the day-to-day work of personnel assigned to the Corporation{{, including personnel employed through {{EOR_LEGAL_NAME}}}}; (b) review and confirm timesheets, time reports and related records for such personnel; (c) act as the Corporation's designated client representative and supervisor under such arrangements; and (d) perform such other duties as the President may assign from time to time.
3. **Limits of authority.** The {{OFFICER_TITLE}} shall have no authority to enter into contracts, incur expenditures or liabilities, open or operate accounts, or otherwise sign on behalf of or bind the Corporation, except as expressly authorized in writing by the President. Confirmation of timesheets and time reports within the scope of paragraph 2 is within the {{OFFICER_TITLE}}'s authority.
4. **Nature of office.** This appointment is an appointment as an officer only. It is not an appointment to the board of directors of the Corporation, and the {{OFFICER_TITLE}} shall not hold himself/herself out as a director within the meaning of the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario).
5. **Remuneration.** The {{OFFICER_TITLE}} shall serve without remuneration unless otherwise agreed in writing by the board of directors.
6. **Records.** The register of officers of the Corporation shall be updated to reflect this appointment, and the President is authorized to notify {{EOR_LEGAL_NAME}} and any other third party of the appointment and of the {{OFFICER_TITLE}}'s contact information ({{officer.email}}@{{DOMAIN}}).
7. **General.** Any officer or director of the Corporation is authorized to execute and deliver all documents and take all actions necessary to give effect to this resolution.

Dated: ____________, {{YEAR}}
_________________________________
**{{FOUNDER_NAME}}**, sole Director
