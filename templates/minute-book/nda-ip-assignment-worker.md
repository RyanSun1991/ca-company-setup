**This Agreement** is made as of {{DATE}} (the "Effective Date"),
**Between:** **{{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}**, a corporation incorporated under the laws of Ontario, with its registered office at {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}} (the "**Company**")
**And:** **{{WORKER_NAME}}**, an individual residing in Ontario (the "**Individual**").

**Whereas** the Individual is employed by {{EOR_LEGAL_NAME}} (the "**Employer**") and has been assigned to perform {{quality and project management}} services for the Company (the "**Assignment**");
**And whereas** in connection with the Assignment the Individual will have access to the Company's confidential information and systems and may create work product for the Company;
**Now therefore**, in consideration of the Company permitting the Individual to participate in the Assignment and granting access to its Confidential Information and systems, the payment of $10.00, and other good and valuable consideration (the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged), the parties agree:

### 1. Definitions
**"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information of or about the Company, in any form, disclosed to or accessed by the Individual in connection with the Assignment, including business plans, product plans and roadmaps, source code, designs, data, user information, financial information, supplier and partner terms, and the terms of this Agreement — but excluding information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Individual; (b) was lawfully known to the Individual before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without duty of confidence; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, provided the Individual gives the Company prompt notice where lawful.
**"Work Product"** means all work results, deliverables, documents, reports, test plans, processes, software, inventions, improvements and other materials, and all intellectual property therein, that the Individual creates, alone or with others, in the course of or in connection with the Assignment.

### 2. Confidentiality
The Individual shall use Confidential Information solely for performing the Assignment, shall not disclose it to any person other than as necessary for the Assignment, and shall protect it with at least reasonable care. This obligation continues during the Assignment and for five (5) years after it ends; for trade secrets and personal information, it continues for as long as the information remains a trade secret or personal information. Upon the end of the Assignment or upon request, the Individual shall return or destroy all Confidential Information in the Individual's possession or control and confirm having done so.

### 3. Assignment of Intellectual Property
The Individual hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest, throughout the world, in and to all Work Product, including all copyright, patent rights, trade secret rights and other intellectual property rights, effective upon creation of each item of Work Product. This assignment applies to Work Product whether created before, on or after the Effective Date, in each case in connection with the Assignment. The Individual shall promptly disclose all Work Product to the Company and shall, at the Company's expense, execute all documents and do all things reasonably necessary to perfect, register or enforce the Company's rights.

### 4. Waiver of Moral Rights
The Individual hereby irrevocably and unconditionally **waives**, in whole, all moral rights (within the meaning of the *Copyright Act* (Canada), including rights of attribution, association and integrity) that the Individual has or may have in any Work Product, in favour of the Company and its successors, assigns, licensees and nominees, each of whom may exercise the benefit of this waiver.

### 5. Personal Information
Where the Assignment involves access to personal information handled by the Company, the Individual shall access only what is authorized and necessary, handle it in accordance with the Company's privacy practices and applicable law (including PIPEDA), and immediately report any suspected loss, unauthorized access or disclosure to the Company.

### 6. No Conflicting Obligations
The Individual represents that entering into and performing this Agreement does not breach any obligation owed to any other person, and agrees not to use in the Assignment any confidential information or intellectual property of any third party without authority.

### 7. Relationship of the Parties
The Individual remains an employee of the Employer. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency or partnership relationship between the Individual and the Company, nor any entitlement to wages, benefits or other compensation from the Company; all compensation for the Assignment is paid by the Employer.

### 8. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect, and the provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted. Sections 2 through 5 survive the end of the Assignment. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supplements (and does not limit) any obligations the Individual owes the Employer in respect of the Company. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronically.
