# By-Law No. 1

**A by-law relating generally to the conduct of the affairs of {{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}** (the "Corporation"), enacted under the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario) (the "Act").

## 1. Interpretation
1.1 In this by-law, "Act" means the *Business Corporations Act*, R.S.O. 1990, c. B.16, as amended; "board" means the board of directors of the Corporation; terms defined in the Act have the same meanings in this by-law.
1.2 Where the Corporation has only one director or one shareholder, any provision of this by-law relating to meetings, quorum or voting shall be read as permitting that single person to exercise all powers of the directors or shareholders, as the case may be, including by resolution in writing under the Act.

## 2. Registered Office
The registered office of the Corporation shall be at the location in Ontario determined from time to time by the board.

## 3. Corporate Seal
The Corporation may, but need not, adopt a corporate seal. An instrument is not invalid merely because it is not sealed.

## 4. Directors
4.1 **Number.** The board shall consist of the number of directors, within the minimum and maximum set out in the articles, determined from time to time by resolution of the shareholders or, where authorized, of the directors.
4.2 **Quorum.** A majority of the directors in office constitutes a quorum; where the Corporation has one director, that director constitutes a quorum.
4.3 **Meetings.** Meetings of the board may be called by any director on not less than forty-eight (48) hours' notice, which notice may be waived. Meetings may be held by telephone or electronic means where all participants can communicate simultaneously.
4.4 **Written resolutions.** A resolution in writing signed by all directors entitled to vote is as valid as if passed at a meeting.
4.5 **Remuneration.** Directors' remuneration, if any, shall be determined by the board.

## 5. Officers
5.1 The board may appoint a President, a Secretary, a Treasurer and such other officers as it determines. One person may hold any number of offices.
5.2 Officers shall have the powers and duties usually incident to their offices and such other powers and duties as the board prescribes. The board may remove any officer and fix officer remuneration.

## 6. Shares
6.1 **Issuance.** Shares may be issued at such times, to such persons and for such consideration as the board determines, subject to the Act and the articles.
6.2 **Certificates.** Every registered holder is entitled to a share certificate or a written acknowledgment of the right to obtain one, signed by at least one officer or director.
6.3 **Transfers.** No transfer of shares shall be registered except in accordance with the restrictions set out in the articles and upon surrender of the certificate (if any) properly endorsed.

## 7. Shareholders' Meetings
7.1 The annual meeting shall be held within fifteen (15) months after the last annual meeting at such time and place as the board determines. Special meetings may be called by the board at any time.
7.2 Notice of meetings shall be given not less than ten (10) and not more than fifty (50) days before the meeting. A quorum is one or more holders of a majority of the shares entitled to vote, present in person or by proxy.
7.3 A resolution in writing signed by all shareholders entitled to vote is as valid as if passed at a meeting.

## 8. Banking and Execution of Instruments
8.1 The banking business of the Corporation shall be transacted with such banks or financial institutions as the board designates, and all such business may be transacted on the Corporation's behalf by such officers or persons as the board authorizes.
8.2 Deeds, transfers, assignments, contracts, obligations, certificates and other instruments may be signed on behalf of the Corporation by any one director or officer. The board may from time to time direct the manner in which any instrument may or shall be signed.

## 9. Financial Year
The financial year of the Corporation shall end on such date as the board determines by resolution.

## 10. Notices
Any notice may be given by personal delivery, mail, courier or electronic means to the recipient's recorded address, and is deemed given when sent (if electronic) or five (5) days after mailing.

## 11. Indemnification
The Corporation shall indemnify each director and officer, and each former director and officer, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act.

## 12. Effective Date
This by-law is effective upon enactment by the board, subject to confirmation, amendment or rejection by the shareholders in accordance with the Act.
