# 股东借款授权决议(Shareholder Loan Authorization)

> **用途**:公司账上只有股本,运营开支(EOR 押金与周发票、保费、设备)由股东提供**免息、无担保、随时可还**的借款——还款免税、无利息申报、一纸决议即合规。
> **签署时机**:**第一笔转账之前**(日期填转账当日或更早)。
> **操作**:个人账户 → 公司 Chequing,e-Transfer 备注 **"Shareholder loan"**;每笔在下方台账记一行。
> ⚠️ 方向:股东借给公司干净;反向(公司借给股东)有 ITA s.15(2) 计入收入风险。累计未偿超过上限先做提额决议。

## Resolution of the Sole Director of {{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}
### (the "Corporation")

**Whereas** the Corporation requires working capital for operations, including payroll services, insurance and general expenses;
**And whereas** {{FOUNDER_NAME}}, the sole director and sole shareholder of the Corporation (the "**Lender**"), is willing to advance funds to the Corporation from time to time;
**And whereas** the Lender has disclosed in writing, by this resolution, the nature and extent of his/her interest in the arrangement authorized hereby, in accordance with section 132 of the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario);

**Be it resolved that:**
1. The Corporation is authorized to borrow from the Lender, in one or more advances from time to time, up to an aggregate principal amount outstanding of **CAD ${{LOAN_CAP}}** (the "**Shareholder Loan**").
2. The Shareholder Loan shall be **non-interest-bearing**, **unsecured**, and **repayable on demand**, provided that no repayment shall be made if, after the repayment, the Corporation would be unable to meet its liabilities as they become due.
3. The terms of the Shareholder Loan, being interest-free to the Corporation, are fair and reasonable to the Corporation and no less favourable than terms obtainable from arm's-length lenders.
4. Each advance shall be recorded in the loan ledger maintained with this resolution, and the books of the Corporation shall record all advances and repayments as shareholder loan transactions.
5. Any one officer or director of the Corporation is authorized to execute and deliver all documents and take all actions necessary to give effect to this resolution.

The undersigned, being the sole director of the Corporation, signs this resolution pursuant to the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario), effective as of the date below.
