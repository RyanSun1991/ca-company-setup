# Assignment of Intellectual Property

**This Assignment** is made as of **{{CLOSING_DATE}}** (the "Effective Date"),

**Between:** **{{FOUNDER_NAME}}**, an individual residing in {{CITY}}, Ontario (the "**Assignor**")
**And:** **{{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}**, a corporation incorporated under the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario), Ontario Corporation Number {{OCN}} (the "**Assignee**")

## Background
A. Prior to and in anticipation of the incorporation of the Assignee, the Assignor created and owns certain software, designs, brands and related intellectual property intended to be owned and commercialized by the Assignee.
B. The Assignor is the sole director and an officer of the Assignee and wishes to assign such intellectual property to the Assignee.

## 1. Assigned IP
The "**Assigned IP**" means all right, title and interest of the Assignor, worldwide, in and to:
(a) the mobile application known as "**{{APP_NAME}}**" (bundle identifier {{BUNDLE_ID}}, App Store Apple ID {{APP_STORE_ID}}), including all source code, object code, build and deployment configurations, backend services and schemas, models and algorithms, designs, user interfaces, artwork, App Store listing materials, and documentation;
(b) the unpublished application known as "**{{APP_NAME_2}}**", including all source code, designs and related materials;
(c) the "**{{BRAND}}**" and "**{{APP_NAME}}**" names, wordmarks, logos and brand assets, the contents of the website {{DOMAIN}}, and all associated goodwill;
(d) all copyright, trade-mark rights, trade secrets, know-how, inventions (whether or not patentable) and all other intellectual property rights subsisting in the foregoing, including the right to sue for past infringement; and
(e) all improvements, modifications and derivative works of the foregoing created by the Assignor on or prior to the Effective Date.
For clarity, the Assigned IP does not include third-party or open-source components, which remain subject to their own licences.

## 2. Assignment
In consideration of the issuance to the Assignor of **one (1) Common Share** in the capital of the Assignee (the "Consideration Share"), the sufficiency of which is acknowledged, the Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers and conveys the Assigned IP to the Assignee, absolutely and worldwide. For clarity, the Consideration Share is separate from, and in addition to, the 100 Common Shares subscribed for in cash by the Assignor pursuant to the directors' resolutions of the Assignee, which cash-subscription shares form no part of the consideration for this Assignment. The parties may jointly elect under section 85 of the *Income Tax Act* (Canada) in respect of any or all of the eligible properties comprised in the Assigned IP, at such elected amount **for each such property** as they may determine within the limits of that section (a schedule identifying the transferred properties and allocating the consideration among them to be prepared for that purpose), and shall execute Form T2057 and any similar provincial form if they so elect.

## 3. Moral Rights Waiver
The Assignor irrevocably waives, in favour of the Assignee and its successors, licensees and assigns, all moral rights in the Assigned IP to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 4. Further Assurances
The Assignor shall execute such further documents and do such further acts as the Assignee reasonably requires to perfect, register or evidence the assignment, including cooperating with the transfer of the related Apple Developer account assets and App Store listings (membership conversion or app transfer, signing identifiers and certificates) to an account controlled by the Assignee.

## 5. Transitional Administration and Revenue
Until the Apple Developer account transfer completes, the Assignor holds and administers the App Store listings and related developer-platform assets as nominee and for the benefit of the Assignee. Allocation of proceeds shall be determined by the **purchase (transaction) date shown in Apple's financial reports**: all revenues, receivables and other proceeds in respect of the applications described in Section 1 arising from transactions (including new subscriptions and renewals) with a purchase date **on or after the Effective Date** — net of Apple's commissions actually charged — belong to the Assignee, and where paid by Apple to the Assignor's developer account shall be received and held by the Assignor in trust for the Assignee and remitted to the Assignee within thirty (30) days after receipt, in the currency received or its Canadian-dollar equivalent, together with the related Apple financial reports reasonably required for the Assignee's accounting and tax reporting. Proceeds from transactions with a purchase date before the Effective Date remain for the account of the Assignor, without proration of subscription periods spanning the Effective Date. Refunds and chargebacks follow the allocation of the original transaction to which they relate.

## 6. Disclosure of Interest
The Assignor, being the sole director and an officer of the Assignee, has disclosed to the Assignee the nature and extent of his/her interest in this Assignment in accordance with section 132 of the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario). The parties consider this Assignment to be fair and reasonable to the Assignee, and it shall be submitted to the shareholder of the Assignee for confirmation and approval.

## 7. General
This Assignment is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein, and binds and benefits the parties and their respective heirs, successors and assigns.
